# Hi there, I'm Julián Pantoja Clavijo

**Atmospheric Geomatics Engineer | Data Scientist | AI for Climate Resilience**

I am a Systems Engineer and Military Tech Lead with 20 years of operational experience in the Colombian Aerospace Force (FAC). My work sits at the intersection of **Numerical Weather Prediction (WRF)**, **Geospatial Analytics**, and **Machine Learning**.

I specialize in building end-to-end data pipelines for atmospheric forecasting and hybrid AI models for Disaster Risk Management. I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Data Science and Analytics, focusing on AI-driven Early Warning Systems for hydrometeorological extremes.

### 🔭 Current Focus
- **M.Sc. Data Science & Analytics (UNAD)**: Developing bias-correction algorithms for operational WRF outputs using Machine Learning.
- **Antarctic Research**: Lead numerical modeler (WRF Polar) for the IX (2022-2023) and X (2023-2025) Colombian Antarctic Scientific Expeditions.
- **Tech Stack Modernization**: Migrating meteorological data processing to robust `xarray` and `dask` pipelines for high-performance computing (HPC).

### 🚀 Featured Research & Projects
* 🏔️ **[antarctic-wrf-polar-analysis](https://github.com/jpantojac/antarctic-wrf-polar-analysis)** *(Planned)*
  *Exploratory Data Analysis and visualization pipeline for WRF Polar simulations in the Antarctic Peninsula.*
* 🌩️ **[wrf-metar-validation-pipeline](https://github.com/jpantojac/wrf-metar-validation-pipeline)** *(Planned)*
  *Automated Python pipeline for extracting, parallelizing, and validating multidimensional NetCDF WRF forecasts against historical METAR station data.*
* 🌍 **[ml-wrf-bias-correction](https://github.com/jpantojac/ml-wrf-bias-correction)** *(In Progress)*
  *Applying Machine Learning to reduce topographic bias in operational WRF outputs for complex Andean terrains.*
